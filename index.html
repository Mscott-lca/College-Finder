College Finder for Tennessee Students

Every U.S. college that takes federal financial aid, with links to apply, financial aid and scholarships, and net-price calculators. Tennessee schools add hand-checked scholarship deadlines and free application periods.

Tennessee deadlines coming up

      Aid every Tennessee student should know

      These apply across schools. Most of them start with the FAFSA, so file it early.

      Applying out of state as a Tennessee resident

      Out-of-state public universities charge non-resident tuition, which is the price shown for schools outside Tennessee. Many of them offer automatic merit scholarships to non-residents that cut that price a lot, so check the scholarships link for each school.

      The Academic Common Market lets Tennesseans pay in-state tuition at public colleges in other southern states for certain majors Tennessee doesn’t offer.

      Other states run their own free application weeks, mostly in October and November. Most of them are limited to that state’s residents, so read the rules before counting on one. The “No application fee” filter shows schools that never charge one. If you have financial need, a Common App, NACAC, ACT or College Board fee waiver covers fees at most schools year-round.

      HOPE and TN Promise only pay at eligible Tennessee schools.

      About the data

      The application, financial aid and net-price calculator links come from each college’s own report to the U.S. Department of Education. When a school hasn’t reported one, the button searches that school’s own website instead. “Full profile” opens the federal College Navigator, which lists graduation rates, net price by family income, enrollment and more.

      Fee waivers, codes and deadlines change every year. Confirm the details on the school’s site before relying on them.
